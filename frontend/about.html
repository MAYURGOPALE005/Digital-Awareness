About CEP

Digital Privacy Awareness for Community Organization is designed to help people understand the risks of online life and build safer digital habits.

Project

This initiative aims to raise awareness among community members about digital privacy, online risks, and secure digital habits. It promotes practical learning, responsible internet use, and confidence in managing personal information safely online.

  • Increase awareness about digital privacy.
  • Educate users about common cyber risks and scams.
  • Promote safe digital habits for everyday life.
  • Encourage responsible use of digital platforms.
  • Measure awareness improvement through surveys and quizzes.

Community Impact

By teaching simple but effective privacy practices, the project helps users become more aware of threats such as phishing, account compromise, oversharing, and unsafe online transactions.

Safer digital habits
Better awareness
Stronger community trust

Project Methodology

Pre-Survey

Assess current digital privacy awareness and understand common habits within the community.

Awareness Session

Share knowledge about privacy risks, safe online practices, and essential protection strategies.

Post-Survey

Collect feedback to measure awareness improvement after education and guidance.

Analysis

Review trends in survey and quiz data to understand strengths and gaps in awareness.

Impact

Use the findings to improve community understanding and encourage safer digital behavior.
