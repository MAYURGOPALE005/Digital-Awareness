Practical Advice

Digital Safety Tips

Small habits can make a big difference in protecting your personal information and online accounts.

Password Tip

Use a strong and unique password for every important account. A password manager can help you create and remember safe passwords without repetition.

OTP Tip

Never share OTPS, UPI PINs, or verification codes with anyone. Legitimate services will never ask you to share them to receive money or recover an account.

Phishing Tip

Verify links before clicking. If a message creates urgency, asks for personal data, or uses a suspicious sender address, treat it as a scam.

Social Media Tip

Review your privacy settings regularly and limit who can see your posts, images, and personal details. Oversharing can expose you to unwanted attention.

App Permission Tip

Only allow permissions that an app truly needs. Remove camera, microphone, contacts, or location access from apps that do not need it.

Public Wi-Fi Tip

Avoid sensitive transactions on unsecured public Wi-Fi. If you must use it, prefer a secure VPN and avoid banking or online payments.

Build Safer Habits Today

Privacy is not a one-time action — it is a habit. Start with one change today and keep improving your digital safety.
